Data sources & limits

  • Forex, gold and news come from the snapshot file data.json, rebuilt by the refresh pipeline (Yahoo and RSS block in-browser requests). A Vantage tick under 12 minutes old is the live price and is tagged Vantage. Otherwise crypto and gold keep updating from Binance.
  • Analyze chart loads Binance candles in the browser for crypto, and PAXG candles as a labelled proxy for gold. FX uses snapshot candles from data.json.
  • Gold is COMEX futures (GC=F), a close proxy for spot XAU/USD. When the scan has a Vantage quote under 15 minutes old, that symbol's price and plan levels are shifted onto the Vantage close.
  • Spot FX has no central volume: OBV and VWAP stay neutral for FX.
